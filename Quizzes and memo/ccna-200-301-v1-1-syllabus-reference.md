# CCNA 200-301 v1.1 Syllabus Reference (2026)
*Official Cisco Exam Topics for Tutoring*

---

## **Exam Overview**
- **Exam Name:** Implementing and Administering Cisco Solutions (CCNA 200-301) v1.1
- **Duration:** 120 minutes
- **Question Types:** Multiple-choice, drag-and-drop, simulations, simlets
- **Passing Score:** Set by Cisco (typically 800-850/1000)
- **Languages:** English, Japanese
- **Cost:** $300 USD (or redeemable with Cisco Learning Credits)

---
